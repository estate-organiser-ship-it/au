The questions people ask every week

Is it Australian?

Yes, written here for here. Super, Centrelink, the state by state differences, the Australian Death Notification Service. It is not an American workbook with the spelling changed.

Do I still need a will?

Yes. This is not a will and it is not legal advice. It is the map that goes with the will. Keep your solicitor.

What if things change?

Use pencil. Update it once a year on a date you will remember. There is a page for that.

Is my information safe?

It is on paper, in your handwriting, in your drawer. Nothing is typed into a website and nothing is stored online. The digital edition lives on your own computer.

Can I do it for my mum?

Yes, and a lot of people do. Sit down together and fill it in one section at a time. It becomes the conversation you kept postponing.

How long does it take?

Section 1 takes about 20 minutes. The whole book is a few evenings, not a project. Nobody does it in one sitting.
