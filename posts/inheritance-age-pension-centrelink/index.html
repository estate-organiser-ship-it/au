Inheritance and the Age Pension: What to Tell Centrelink, and When

Published 26 November 2026 by Will Kimak.

If you get the Age Pension and money or property comes to you from a deceased estate, Services Australia says you must tell it within 14 days of getting it. Generally it won't assess what you are entitled to until you get it, are able to get it or benefit from it, and from that point the inheritance can be counted in your income and assets tests. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026)

When does an inheritance start to count?

Being named in a will is not the trigger. Services Australia says it understands that it can take some time to finalise an estate, and that generally it won't assess any assets you are entitled to until one of three things happens:

  • you get them
  • you are able to get them
  • you benefit from them.

The 14 days runs from getting the assets or income. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026)

A partner's death works a little differently. If you owned assets jointly with a partner who has died, Services Australia says those assets legally transfer to you, and you must tell it within 14 days when that happens. Assets your partner owned outright are part of their estate, and Services Australia won't assess them until you get them or are able to get them. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026)

How an inheritance can change the pension

There are two tests, and an inheritance can touch both.

The assets test. Services Australia assesses all asset types in the assets test. How much it can pay depends on the value of your assets, whether you own your home and whether you are in a relationship. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026) These are the current limits from its assets test page:

Your situationFull pension limitPart pension cut off
Single homeowner$333,000$745,750
Single non-homeowner$600,000$1,012,750
Couple homeowner, combined$499,000$1,121,000
Couple non-homeowner, combined$766,000$1,388,000

Above the full pension limit, the pension reduces. From 20 September 2026, part pensions cancel when assets are over the cut off point. The Department of Social Services reviews these limits in March, July and September each year, so check the page on the day you need it. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026)

The income test. Inherited cash in a bank account, a term deposit or shares is a financial asset, and Services Australia uses deeming to work out the income it creates, whatever it really earns. For a single person, the first $66,800 of financial assets is deemed to earn 1.75% and everything over that 3.75%. For a couple where at least one gets a pension, the first $110,600 of combined financial assets is deemed at 1.75% and the rest at 3.75%. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026)

An inherited house. Services Australia includes most real estate you own in the assets test. The only real estate it leaves out is your principal home, so a second house you rent out or leave vacant is counted. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026)

What happens to the Pensioner Concession Card?

Services Australia sends a Pensioner Concession Card to people who get certain payments, and Age Pension is one of them. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026) Card holders must tell it if their own or their partner's income or assets change, and the change needs to be reported within 14 days. It also says it will cancel the card if you stop getting a Centrelink payment. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026)

So the card follows the payment. While you still get the Age Pension, you are in the group the card goes to. If the pension stops, the card is cancelled. Services Australia also warns that if you don't tell it about changes, it may pay you too much, and you will have to pay the money back, plus a fee. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026)

Five steps when an inheritance arrives

  1. Write down the date the money or property reached you, or the date you were able to get it.
  2. Report it within 14 days. Services Australia says you can update your income and assets through your Centrelink online account at any time. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026)
  3. Report a joint asset that has passed to you from a partner within 14 days of the transfer.
  4. Keep a copy of what you reported and when.
  5. Pause before giving any of it away. See the next section.

What if you want to pass some of it to the kids?

Services Australia says giving away an asset you owned jointly with the person who died, or one you got from their estate, is gifting, and that this can affect your payment. It suggests talking to its Financial Information Service officers first. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026) The gifting free areas are $10,000 in one financial year and $30,000 over 5 financial years, with no more than $10,000 of that in a single year. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026) Our guide to Centrelink gifting rules walks through how those limits work.

The Financial Information Service is a free service that can inform and educate you about financial matters. (Source: servicesaustralia.gov.au, checked 16 Nov 2026) It is a sensible first call before a big decision about inherited money.

Frequently asked questions

How long do I have to tell Centrelink about an inheritance?

Services Australia says you must tell it within 14 days of getting any assets or income from a deceased estate. If assets you owned jointly with a partner who died transfer to you, you must also tell it within 14 days.

Does Centrelink count an inheritance before I receive it?

Generally, no. Services Australia says it won't assess assets you are entitled to from an estate until you get them, are able to get them or benefit from them.

Will an inheritance stop my Age Pension?

It depends on your total assets and income. Above the full pension limit the pension reduces, and from 20 September 2026 part pensions cancel when assets are over the cut off point, which is $745,750 for a single homeowner and $1,121,000 combined for a homeowner couple.

What happens to my Pensioner Concession Card after an inheritance?

Card holders must tell Services Australia within 14 days if their income or assets change. Services Australia says it will cancel the card if you stop getting a Centrelink payment.

Can I give my inheritance to my children?

Services Australia treats giving away an asset you got from an estate as gifting, which can affect your payment. The gifting free areas are $10,000 in one financial year and $30,000 over 5 financial years. It suggests talking to its Financial Information Service first.
