# Sources: Probate Checklist Australia: How to Apply, State by State

Site: au. Slug: `probate-checklist-australia`. Published 29 September 2026.
Source pack: `research/03-probate-checklist-au.md`.

**Every dollar figure in this post was independently re-confirmed against the live
registry page or PDF on 26 September 2026, after the pack was written.** Court fee
scales in Australia generally change on 1 July each year, so every figure in the post
carries an explicit "as at the date this was checked" frame and links to the registry's
own fee page.

## 4. What was CUT for lack of a source

1. **Any dollar threshold at which probate becomes compulsory**, national or per state.
 No registry publishes one. The post says so explicitly rather than hedging.
2. **Small-estate dollar definitions** (NSW Probate and Administration Act Div 4,
 VIC s 71 and the $285.00 small-estate preparation fee). The provisions exist but the
 dollar definitions were never read, so no threshold is stated and the VIC small-estate
 fee is not quoted, because quoting it without its definition would mislead.
3. **WA, SA and TAS advertising requirements and waiting periods.** Not stated on the
 pages read. The post names the three states, says the requirement was not confirmed,
 and links to each registry. It does NOT say these states have no notice requirement.
4. **Processing times for NSW, QLD, WA, TAS, ACT and NT.** The NSW turnaround page
 returned 404. Only VIC and SA figures are published.
5. **Original will handling in NSW, QLD and TAS**, and whether the court retains it
 anywhere outside VIC and SA. Called out as unconfirmed in the post.
6. **The Queensland Law Reporter advertising cost.** Charged by ICLRQ, not the court, and
 no amount was read. The post says the charge exists and is separate, with no figure.
7. **NSW notice of intended distribution: the outer time limit** for filing after the
 intended-application notice. Only the 14-day minimum is verified.
8. **The Tasmanian "no waiver, reduction, postponement or refund" sentence.** The pack
 carried this, but a live re-check of the TAS fees page found no such sentence anywhere
 in the page text. Removed from the draft.
9. **All 2025 fee figures found on disk** in
 `Estate Organiser/Search-Posts-2026-09/content_au.py`. Every NSW and VIC band in that
 file is superseded. None was used.
