## Cut for lack of a source

- **Wills, marriage and divorce in Western Australia and South Australia.** The
  pack could not confirm either from a state government page. The post names both
  states as not covered rather than implying the Tasmanian or NSW rule is national.
- **State by state naming of advance care directives beyond VIC, NSW and QLD.**
  The national aggregator returned a bot-check on every fetch, so SA and WA
  document names are absent. The post shows the three verified names and links the
  federal page that says the forms vary.
- **Any claim that enduring guardianship or medical decision-maker appointments
  are not recognised interstate.** Only the financial EPA cross-recognition rule
  was verified, so only that is stated, and only for NSW.
- **A Queensland tribunal (QCAT) applicant-facing statement** matching the VCAT and
  NCAT lines. Not on the fetched page, so Queensland is represented by the Public
  Guardian's "risks of not having an EPOA and AHD" instead, and no QCAT claim is made.
- **The SMSF 5 year binding nomination lapse period.** Not in the fetched ATO or
  Moneysmart text. Only the 3 year figure Moneysmart states is used.
- **All dollar figures for aged care means-tested fees and thresholds.** None were
  read with an "as at" date. The post points the reader to the live My Aged Care
  page instead.
- **A dedicated NSW Public Guardian advice line number.** The old domain no longer
  resolves. Replaced with the verified NSW Trustee and Guardian general line.
