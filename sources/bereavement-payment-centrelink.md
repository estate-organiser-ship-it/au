## Cut or not used

- A dollar amount for the partner bereavement payment: Services Australia gives only the method (couple rate minus single rate over 14 weeks), so no figure is stated.
- The phone line some third-party pages describe as a 'bereavement' option on 132 300: the Services Australia phone page lists 132 300 as the older Australians line, so the post describes it only that way.
- Rates for the Pension Bonus Bereavement Payment: the amount page returned 404 today, so the post gives only the basis of the payment.
